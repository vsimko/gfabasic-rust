## Reconciliation notes

- Preserve separate entries where the printed index distinguishes a statement, function, property, or assignment form.
- OCR forms such as `DIM?()`, `FLOAT{}`, `JWORD{}`, `INT{}`, `QADR()`, `OBJECT.VX/VX()`, and `VAL?()` require image-level checking before normalization.
- The OCR sometimes duplicates entries or loses punctuation. The original printed index should be checked directly before merging aliases.
- The page numbers in Appendix D are references to printed book pages, not PDF page numbers.
- Presence in this index is not evidence that an item belongs to the 3.5 addendum. Chapter 18 is the explicit 3.5 section; chapter 19 needs individual version attribution.
